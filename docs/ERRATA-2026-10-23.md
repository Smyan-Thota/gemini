# Results correction

The version 1 and version 2 archives remain unchanged as historical artifacts.

Their RESULTS text incorrectly says both models completed 0/12 adversarial episodes. The saved independent score for the visible-read Qwen-alone run reports **1/12 completed**, with **2 unauthorized committed uploads**. The paired V7 run reports **0/12 completed**, with **0 unauthorized committed uploads**. Preventing those effects therefore did not establish useful task completion.

The standard visible-read comparison remains **11/16 completed for Qwen alone** and **6/16 for Qwen plus V7**.

The V8 run-2 receipt incorrectly reports 576 optimizer updates completed. Its early-stop branch assigns the planned total to the step counter before writing the receipt. The actual recorded evaluations/checkpoints end at **192 updates**, with evaluations at 64, 128, and 192. No later checkpoints were omitted from selection.

These corrections concern reporting and documentation. They do not qualify any V8 checkpoint for release. The V7 package remains experimental and fails the deployment-utility target.

These corrections were recomputed from retained independent score records and the V8 training receipt and early-stop logic. Raw records remain private.
